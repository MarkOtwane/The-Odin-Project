Beef Recipe

Home

Description

Beef is highly nutritious, acting as a rich source of high-quality protein, iron, and vitamin B12

Ingredients

  • 1 kg beef chuck
  • The Aromatics
  • Vegges
  • Liquid Base
  • Thickeners and fat

Steps

  1. Pat the beef cubes completely dry with paper towels (this ensures a good crust). Season generously with salt and black pepper.
  2. Heat the olive oil or butter in your pot over medium-high heat. Add the beef in a single layer—do not overcrowd the pan (work in batches if necessary). Sear for 2–3 minutes per side until a deep, brown crust forms. Remove the beef and set it aside on a plate.
  3. Add the chopped onion and sliced celery. Sauté for 5 minutes until the onions become soft and translucent.
  4. Stir in the minced garlic and tomato paste. Cook for another 1–2 minutes, stirring constantly until the paste darkens slightly.
  5. Add the bay leaves and fresh thyme sprigs. Bring the liquid to a boil.
  6. Once boiling, cover the pot with the lid and turn the heat down to low. Let it simmer gently for 1 hour and 30 minutes. The beef should begin to feel tender if poked with a fork.
  7. Stir in the carrot chunks and halved baby potatoes.
  8. Cover the pot again and continue to simmer on low for another 45 to 60 minutes, or until the vegetables are fork-tender and the beef is melt-in-your-mouth soft.
  9. Remove the bay leaves and thyme stems. Taste the gravy and add more salt or pepper if needed. Serve hot with crusty bread.
