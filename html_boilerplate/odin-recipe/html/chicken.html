Chicken

Home

Juicy, flavorful chicken cooked to perfection, a versatile dish that can be enjoyed in various cuisines.

Ingredients

  • Chicken
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Cooking Oil

Steps

  1. Season the chicken with salt and pepper.
  2. Heat cooking oil in a pan over medium heat.
  3. Add the chicken to the pan and cook for about 6-7 minutes on each side, or until the internal temperature reaches 165°F (75°C).
  4. Remove the chicken from the pan and let it rest for a few minutes before serving.

Serve as a main dish with your favorite sides.
