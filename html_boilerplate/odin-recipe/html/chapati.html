Chapati

Soft, flaky chapatis made from basic ingredients, cooked to golden perfection.

ingredients

  • Wheat Flour
  • Warm Water
  • Salt
  • Sugar
  • Cooking Oil
  • Carrots

Steps

  1. In a bowl, mix wheat flour, salt, sugar, and grated carrots
  2. Add warm water gradually and knead into a soft dough.
  3. Add a little cooking oil and knead again until smooth.
  4. Cover the dough and let it rest for 10–15 minutes.
  5. Divide the dough into small balls.
  6. Roll each ball into a thin circle
  7. Roll each ball into a thin circle.
  8. Heat a pan and cook each chapati until golden on both sides.
  9. Brush lightly with oil and serve hot.

Serve hot with beef stew or chicken.
