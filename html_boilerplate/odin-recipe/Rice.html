Rice Recipe

Home

Fluffy, flavorful rice cooked to perfection, a versatile side dish that complements any meal.

Ingredients

  • Rice
  • Water
  • Salt
  • Cooking Oil

Steps

  1. Rinse the rice under cold water until the water runs clear.
  2. In a pot, bring water to a boil and add salt.
  3. Add the rinsed rice to the boiling water.
  4. Reduce heat to low, cover the pot, and simmer for about 15-20 minutes until the rice is tender and water is absorbed.
  5. Remove from heat and let it sit covered for 5 minutes.
  6. Fluff the rice with a fork before serving.

Serve as a side dish with your favorite main course.
